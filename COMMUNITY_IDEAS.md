## Easy remixes

Take any idea above and change one thing:

- **Location:** motel, casino, airport, mall, office, desert, marina, warehouse
- **Job:** manager, security guard, salesman, reporter, driver, detective, technician
- **Mood:** serious, exhausted, luxurious, suspicious, calm, chaotic
- **Format:** IMAGE, MINI, SCENE, BUMPER, FAKE AD
- **Time:** sunrise, high noon, sunset, midnight, rainy night

The same Chihuahua can live in hundreds of different worlds. The fun is seeing what everyone invents.
