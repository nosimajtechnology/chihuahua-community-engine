# Chihuahua Community Guide

## What is this?

The Chihuahua Community Engine is a creative Skill.

It helps you make images, short scenes, mini animations, loops, and fake
commercials with the pink-durag Chihuahua.

The Chihuahua reference and old-game style rules are already inside.

## Do I need to know prompting?

No.

Write one normal sentence:

> Chihuahua works the night shift at a gas station.

The Engine handles the character, camera, game graphics, and prompt.

## How do I install it?

This works in regular ChatGPT on mobile and desktop.

1. Unzip the full Chihuahua Community Engine package.
2. Open the folder you just unzipped.
3. Find `1_INSTALL_CHIHUAHUA_ENGINE.zip`. **Leave this ZIP zipped.**
4. In ChatGPT, open **Plugins** from the sidebar.
5. Open **Skills**, then choose **Create** and **Upload from your computer**.
6. Select `1_INSTALL_CHIHUAHUA_ENGINE.zip`.
7. Start a new **Chat** or **Work** session.

Use this as your first prompt:

> Load the “Chihuahua Community Engine” skill.

Then describe your idea.

## Can I only make images?

Yes. IMAGE is the simplest mode.

Try:

> Make one image of Chihuahua eating alone at a fancy restaurant. No video.

It will not make a storyboard unless you ask.

## How do I make a video?

Describe an action:

> Chihuahua gets chased through an airport.

The Engine may make:

1. a starting image
2. a connected storyboard
3. a video prompt for your model

Approve each visual before moving on.

- jobs

- boring errands
- mysteries
- sports
- vehicles
- restaurants
- airports
- motels

- offices

- commercials
- loading screens
- character-select screens

## How do I change Chihuahua's clothes?

Say what he should wear:

> Keep everything else. Give him a black tracksuit.

The Engine should keep his face, size, eyes, and pink durag unless you ask to
change them.

## Can I make my own story?

Yes.

Your story can be serious, funny, strange, quiet, or dramatic.

The best results use one clear idea at a time.

## What does “Approved” mean?

It means:

> Keep this version. Use it for the next step.

After you approve an image, the Engine uses it to keep Chihuahua, the outfit,
the place, and the game graphics consistent.

After you approve a storyboard, the Engine keeps that shot order for animation.

## What if something looks wrong?

Say exactly what failed:

> He turned into a normal dog.

> The durag disappeared.

> Shot 2 changed the car.

> One eye is wrong. Fix only the eye.

The Engine will protect the parts that already work.

## Which video models can I use?

The Engine can prepare prompts for:

- Seedance
- Kling
- another image-to-video model

Model screens and controls change. If you are not sure, ask for a general
image-to-video prompt.

## Do I need to mention the token?

No.

Chihuahua can exist without logos, tickers, charts, or crypto jokes.

Only add token references when they belong in your idea.

## Can I share what I make?

Yes. Follow the rules of the platform you post on and be careful with any
third-party characters, brands, music, or logos you add.

Simple credit:

> Chihuahua character by Nosimaj Media. Community-created scene.

You do not need to place a watermark inside every image.

## Is my creation official canon?

No. Community creations are unofficial by default.

Community jokes and stories may become popular. Nosimaj may choose to reference
an idea later. The Engine does not decide what becomes official.

## One final rule

Bring your own ideas.

Same Chihuahua. Different minds.
