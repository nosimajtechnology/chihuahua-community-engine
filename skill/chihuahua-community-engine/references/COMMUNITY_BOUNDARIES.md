# Community Boundaries

Keep the Skill focused on Chihuahua media creation.

## Three layers

### Nosimaj Media

Treat Nosimaj Media as the source of the original Chihuahua character and
canonical creative direction.

### Community

Allow unofficial images, memes, stories, scenes, jobs, businesses, clothing,
vehicles, supporting characters, genres, jokes, and emergent lore.

Do not automatically call a community creation official canon. Use language
such as `community-created`, `unofficial`, `remix`, or `community lore` when the
distinction matters.

### Community token

Treat any community token as an independent economic layer. The creative Skill
does not control, operate, endorse, price, or promise outcomes for it.

## Token independence

Do not add these unless the user asks:

- token logos or ticker symbols
- charts or price screens
- crypto jokes
- trading language
- financial promises
- community-token promotion

`Chihuahua at a restaurant` should remain an ordinary character scene.

Allow logos or token references as requested creative elements when otherwise
safe. Keep them subordinate to the requested scene.

## Financial boundary

Do not use this Skill to provide:

- token trading advice
- investment recommendations
- price targets
- liquidity or market-manipulation tactics
- promises of return
- claims that creating or sharing media will increase value

If a request mixes creative production with a financial claim, help with the
creative asset while separating or declining the unsupported claim.

## Attribution

When attribution is useful, recommend:

> Chihuahua character by Nosimaj Media. Community-created scene.

Do not force the line into the image. Do not automatically watermark every
creation. Let users add platform captions or credits outside the generated
visual.

## Remix philosophy

Aim for:

> same recognizable Chihuahua, different people's ideas

Encourage variation in situations, outfits, places, occupations, vehicles,
supporting cast, genre, and story. Keep core identity anchored.

Do not expose or claim access to Nosimaj's private generalized production
engine, unrelated character systems, advanced commercial playbooks, private
prompt libraries, music choices, editing process, or canon decisions.

## Sharing and canon

Users may share what they create subject to the rules of the platform and any
rights that apply to third-party material they add. Do not give a legal
guarantee. If they use another brand, celebrity, game character, song, or logo,
keep that third-party material clearly separate from the Chihuahua character
attribution.

Nosimaj may choose to reference or canonize a community idea later. Never imply
that this happened without an explicit source decision.
