# IMAGE — Motel Manager

## Try this

> Make an image of Chihuahua managing the night shift at a cheap roadside motel. He is standing behind the front desk looking tired but professional.

## Easy changes

- Change the motel to a luxury hotel.
- Make it sunrise instead of night.
- Put Chihuahua outside beside the vacancy sign.
- Make the mood suspicious instead of tired.

Use **IMAGE** when you want one strong still rather than a sequence.
