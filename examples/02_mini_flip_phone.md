# MINI — Flip Phone Call

## Try this

> Make a MINI where Chihuahua is relaxing in a luxury outdoor hot tub. His flip phone rings, he answers it, and his expression suddenly turns serious like he just heard bad news.

## Easy changes

- Move the scene to a rooftop pool.
- Replace the hot tub with a hotel balcony.
- Make the call confusing instead of serious.
- End with Chihuahua standing up abruptly.

Use **MINI** for one short animated beat with a clear change or reaction.
