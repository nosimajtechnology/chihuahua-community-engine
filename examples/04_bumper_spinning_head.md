# BUMPER — Spinning Head

## Try this

> Make a BUMPER with Chihuahua's PS2-style head slowly spinning against a minimal dark background. Keep the motion simple and loopable.

## Easy changes

- Change the background to stars.
- Add a subtle durag color shift.
- Use a brighter game-menu background.
- Make the rotation slower and stranger.

Use **BUMPER** for short loops, intermissions, IDs, and simple visual fragments.
