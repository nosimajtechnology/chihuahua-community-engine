# Community Transmission Prompts

Lightweight creative prompts for community participation. No prizes or token activity required.

## Transmission 001 — Wrong Place

**Prompt:** Put Chihuahua somewhere he absolutely should not be.

Examples: operating room waiting area, luxury boardroom, abandoned mall, airport runway, medieval castle, local news desk.

Suggested X post:

> CHIHUAHUA TRANSMISSION_001
>
> put chihuahua somewhere he absolutely should not be
>
> use the community engine if you want. post what you make below 🫡

## Transmission 002 — Day Job

**Prompt:** Give Chihuahua a completely normal job and treat it with complete seriousness.

Examples: motel manager, security guard, mechanic, substitute teacher, weather reporter.

## Transmission 003 — Local Commercial

**Prompt:** Make a fake early-2000s commercial for a business Chihuahua owns.

Examples: insurance agency, pawn shop, used cars, limousine service, questionable electronics.

## Transmission 004 — After Midnight

**Prompt:** Show what Chihuahua is doing somewhere between 2 AM and 4 AM.

Examples: empty mall, motel desk, gas station, rooftop, warehouse, diner.

## Transmission 005 — Boss Encounter

**Prompt:** Turn Chihuahua into a boss encounter from a lost PS2 game.

The goal is participation, not competition. Keep prompts broad enough that different creators produce visibly different interpretations.
